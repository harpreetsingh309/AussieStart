Using the app

Does AussieStart need an internet connection?

No. Guides are stored on the device. Safari is used only if you tap an official .gov.au link.

How do I change language, state, or persona?

Open Settings. Language, state, and “I am a” update Home and First 30 Days. Official names such as TFN and Medicare stay in English.

Can the app lodge my visa, TFN, or Medicare for me?

No. AussieStart is informational only. Complete those steps on the official government website.

AussieStart Pro

What is Pro?

A one-time unlock (not a subscription) for extra settlement guides, plus share/print of First 30 Days and optional weekly reminders on this device. Price is shown in the app (AUD $9.99 in Australia).

I paid but Pro guides are still locked

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID that bought Pro. Then reopen the guide.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Email us if you need help describing the issue.

Content corrections

Rules change. If a guide is out of date, email the article title and the official page you compared it with. Always verify critical steps with Services Australia, the ATO, Home Affairs, and your state agency.

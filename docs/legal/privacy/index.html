Privacy Policy

AussieStart · Last updated 19 August 2026 · Publisher: RHK Solutions PTY LTD

Home Support Terms

AussieStart does not require an account. Settlement data stays on your device. We do not run a server that receives it.

What stays on your device

The following are stored only on the iPhone or iPad using the app:

  • Language, Australian state, and persona chosen in onboarding
  • Bookmarks, reading history, and search history
  • Checklist ticks and First 30 Days progress
  • Appearance (light / dark / system)
  • Whether weekly First 30 Days reminders are enabled

Deleting the app removes this data. There is no cloud backup from AussieStart.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or account
  • No advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs
  • No location, camera, contacts, or microphone use
  • No government logins (myGov, ImmiAccount, Centrelink, ATO, Medicare)
  • No visa, TFN, or health records — we cannot see or lodge those

Purchases

If you buy AussieStart Pro, Apple processes the payment under Apple’s privacy policy. The app stores an App Store entitlement on this device so Pro guides unlock. We do not receive your card number or Apple ID password.

Product ID: com.aussiestart.app.pro — one-time non-consumable.

Notifications

Optional weekly reminders are scheduled locally with Apple’s notification APIs. They are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off in Settings.

Links to official sites

Guides include links to Australian Government and state websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. AussieStart never signs in for you.

Not a government service

AussieStart is an independent information app. We cannot see, change, or apply for visas, myGov, TFN, Medicare, Centrelink, licences, or bonds. Use official .gov.au websites for those steps.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.

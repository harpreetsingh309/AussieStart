# Overview
Your Tax File Number (TFN) is your tax ID in Australia.

# Why this matters
Without a TFN, employers may withhold tax at a higher rate.

# When to do it
As soon as you have a local address and phone — often Day 2–5.

# Requirements
- Passport and visa details
- Australian address
- Local phone number and email

# Step-by-step guide
1. Create a myGov account if needed.
2. Apply for a TFN online via the ATO / myGov pathway that matches your visa.
3. Keep your reference number.
4. When issued, store it securely — never post it publicly.
5. Give it to your employer via their official form only.

# Common mistakes
- Sharing your TFN in Facebook groups
- Delaying the application until after starting work
- Confusing TFN with ABN

# FAQs
**Is TFN the same as ABN?** No. ABN is for businesses/sole traders.
**Does it cost money?** Applying for a TFN is free via official channels.

# Useful terms
- **PAYG**: Pay As You Go tax withholding from wages
- **myGov**: Government services portal

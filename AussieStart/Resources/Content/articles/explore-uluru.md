# Overview
Uluru–Kata Tjuta in the Red Centre is a powerful cultural and landscape experience — best as a short flight + overnight stay.

# Why this matters
One of Australia’s most meaningful destinations; plan with respect for Anangu cultural guidance.

# When to do it
April–September is cooler for walks. Summer days can be extreme — start walks at dawn.

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
- **Flights to Ayers Rock (Connellan) or Alice Springs**: $250–$700+ pp return depending on city/season
- **Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park pass (approx.)**: often ~$38 adult for multi-day validity — confirm Parks Australia
- **Airport transfers / hire car**: $40–$120/day range
- **Uluru sunset / field of light style experiences**: $50–$200+ pp depending on product
- **Kata Tjuta (Valley of the Winds) self-guided**: park pass covers entry; bring water
- **Resort meals at Yulara**: $30–$60 pp easily
- **Camel tours / cultural centres (optional)**: $80–$150 pp

# Short itinerary (2 days)
**Day 1:** Arrive Yulara → Uluru base views / cultural centre → sunset viewing area  
**Day 2:** Dawn at Uluru or Kata Tjuta walk → fly out afternoon

# Stops to explore in between
- **Yulara Town Square** — supplies, dining
- **Uluru base viewpoints** (follow marked areas; climbing is not permitted)
- **Mutitjulu Waterhole** area (as signed / guided options)
- **Kata Tjuta dunes lookout**
- **Walpa Gorge** (shorter Kata Tjuta walk alternative)

# Common mistakes
- Underestimating heat and carrying too little water
- Trying to “do everything” at midday
- Ignoring cultural photography restrictions at sacred sites

# FAQs
**Can I climb Uluru?** No — the climb is permanently closed.
**Is Alice Springs the same trip?** Alice is ~450 km away; most visitors fly into Ayers Rock Airport for Uluru stays.

# Useful terms
- **Anangu**: Traditional owners of the area
- **Tjukurpa**: Cultural law / creation stories guiding how Country is cared for

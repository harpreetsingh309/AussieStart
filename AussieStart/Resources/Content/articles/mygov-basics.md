# Overview
myGov is the Australian Government’s online account for many services (ATO, Medicare, Centrelink). This guide explains how people usually set it up — AussieStart does **not** create accounts or lodge anything for you.

# Why this matters
A working myGov account is how you later link the ATO (TFN), Medicare, and other services. Without it, many follow-up steps take longer.

# When to do it
After you have a local phone number and a stable email. Students and workers usually do this in week 1; tourists often do not need myGov.

# Requirements
- Email address you can access
- Australian mobile number (strongly recommended for codes)
- Passport / visa details as used on official forms
- Time to complete identity steps on the **official** website only

# Step-by-step guide
1. Open the official myGov website on your own phone or computer (search “myGov Australia” and check the address is a `.gov.au` site).
2. Create an account with your email, then confirm the code sent to you.
3. Set a strong password and turn on extra sign-in protection if offered.
4. Later, **link** services (such as ATO) only from inside myGov when you are ready — each service has its own identity checks.
5. Never share your myGov password, SMS codes, or recovery email with anyone, including “helpers” who message you first.

# Common mistakes
- Using a lookalike website that is not `.gov.au`
- Giving codes to someone who calls claiming to be from myGov or the ATO
- Creating an account with an email you will lose after changing phones

# FAQs
**Does AussieStart log into myGov?** No. We only explain the usual order of steps. You always use the official site.
**Do I need myGov as a visitor?** Usually no, unless you are working and need ATO access.

# Useful terms
- **Linking**: Connecting myGov to a specific agency (for example the ATO)
- **CRN**: Customer Reference Number used by Services Australia

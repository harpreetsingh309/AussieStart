# Overview
Phillip Island is a favourite Melbourne escape — coastal drives, wildlife, and the Penguin Parade at dusk.

# Why this matters
Close enough for a day trip, rewarding as an overnight if you want sunset penguins without rushing.

# When to do it
Penguin Parade timing follows sunset — check nightly times. Weekdays are calmer than school holidays.

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
- **Fuel Melbourne return**: $50–$90
- **Penguin Parade general viewing**: often ~$30–$45 adult (premium seating higher)
- **3 Parks Pass** (Penguins + Koala + Churchill optional combo): check current bundle pricing
- **Koale Conservation Reserve**: ~$20 adult range if separate
- **Nobbies / boardwalk**: free outdoor areas; visitor centre extras vary
- **Day tour from Melbourne**: $150–$220 pp
- **Fish & chips at San Remo / Cowes**: $20–$35 pp

# Short itinerary (1 day)
1. Depart Melbourne late morning via Monash / Bass Highway
2. **San Remo** bridge views + lunch
3. **Nobbies** boardwalk and blowhole lookouts
4. Optional **Koala Conservation Reserve**
5. **Penguin Parade** at dusk
6. Drive back to Melbourne (arrive late)

# Stops to explore in between
- **Tooradin / Koo Wee Rup** (drive-through towns)
- **San Remo** — pelican feeding times sometimes advertised locally
- **Cowes** — main island town foreshore
- **Nobbies Centre** cliffs
- **Cape Woolamai** (if daylight remains) — surf beach lookout
- **Rhyll** wetlands viewpoints

# Common mistakes
- Arriving after penguin entry cut-off
- Using flash photography (not allowed at penguins)
- Forgetting a warm jacket for evening sea wind

# FAQs
**Are penguins guaranteed?** Wild animals — sightings are common but not promised.
**Can kids do it?** Yes — bring quiet voices and warm layers.

# Useful terms
- **Parade**: Evening return of little penguins to burrows
- **Boardwalk**: Elevated path protecting fragile coastline

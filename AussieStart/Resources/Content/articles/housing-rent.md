# Overview
Most new arrivals rent first — share house, studio, or apartment — using sites like Domain, Flatmates, and realestate.com.au.

# Why this matters
A stable address unlocks banking, licences, and school enrolment.

# When to do it
Start researching before you arrive; inspect and apply in week 1–2.

# Requirements
Often requested:
- ID and visa
- Proof of funds / employment / enrolment
- References
- Bond money (commonly 4 weeks rent) plus rent in advance

# Step-by-step guide
1. Decide suburb based on work/uni and transport.
2. Shortlist listings and book inspections.
3. Submit applications with complete documents.
4. Read the lease before signing — note break fees and inclusions.
5. Complete condition report with photos on move-in day.

# Common mistakes
- Paying bond to an individual without receipts
- Skipping the condition report
- Ignoring rental scams that ask for money before inspection

# FAQs
**What is bond?** A security deposit lodged with the state bond authority — not kept by the landlord personally in most cases.
**Can I rent without a job?** Possible with proof of funds / guarantor, but harder.

# Useful terms
- **Lease**: Rental agreement
- **Inspection**: Viewing the property before applying

# Official links
Check your state tenancy authority for rights and bond lodgement.

# Overview
A rental bond is money you pay as security, then (if all goes well) get back when you leave. In most states it must be lodged with an **official bond authority** — not kept casually by a landlord. AussieStart does not lodge bonds.

# Why this matters
Paying “bond” to a personal account without a receipt is a common scam. Lodging correctly protects you and the owner.

# When to do it
When you sign a lease and the agreement says a bond is due — not before you have a genuine property and written terms.

# Requirements
- Signed residential tenancy agreement
- Photo ID and sometimes a TFN or bank details for refunds
- The official lodgement process for {{state.name}}

# Step-by-step guide
1. Confirm the bond amount in the written lease (there are legal maximums — check your state tenancy site).
2. Pay using the method the **bond authority** or agent specifies. Keep receipts.
3. Complete a condition report with photos on the day you move in.
4. When you leave, clean to a fair standard and agree the refund path in writing.
5. If you disagree, use the official dispute pathway for your state — not social media threats.

# Common mistakes
- Transferring bond to a WhatsApp “agent” you have not met
- Skipping the condition report
- Forgetting to update your forwarding address for the refund

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart hold my bond?** No, and neither should a random individual. Use the state authority.
**Is this legal advice?** No. Tenancy laws differ; read your state site or a tenancy advice service.

# Useful terms
- **Condition report**: Room-by-room record of the property at move-in
- **Lodgement number**: Proof the bond sits with the official authority

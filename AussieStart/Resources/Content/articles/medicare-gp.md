# Overview
Healthcare in Australia mixes public Medicare services and private options. A GP (general practitioner) is your first stop for non-emergencies.

# Why this matters
Knowing where to go — GP, urgent care, or 000 — saves money and time.

# When to do it
In your first 1–2 weeks, find a nearby clinic and understand your visa’s Medicare eligibility.

# Requirements
- Medicare card if eligible for your visa
- Photo ID
- Address and phone number
- List of medicines / allergies

# Step-by-step guide
1. Check Medicare eligibility for your visa subclass.
2. If eligible, enrol and wait for your card.
3. Search for a bulk-billing or mixed-billing GP near you.
4. Book online or by phone; ask about fees before the visit.
5. For medicines, take prescriptions to a chemist (pharmacy).

# Common mistakes
- Going to hospital ED for minor issues (long waits, not always free)
- Assuming every GP visit is free
- Skipping travel/OVHC insurance when your visa requires it

# FAQs
**What is bulk billing?** The clinic charges Medicare directly — no gap fee for eligible patients (not all clinics do this).
**What number is emergency?** Call **000** for police, fire, or ambulance in a life-threatening emergency.

# Useful terms
- **GP**: Family doctor / primary care
- **PBS**: Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (cheaper medicines for eligible people)
- **OVHC**: Overseas Visitor Health Cover

# Overview
Some jobs, volunteer roles, and licences ask for a **national police check** and/or a **Working with Children / vulnerable people check**. You apply on official AFP or **state** portals. AussieStart does not collect ID, fingerprints, or submit checks.

# Why this matters
Paid “fast police check” ads often sit in front of the real form and add junk fees. Use the government page that matches the check type.

# When to do it
When a written job or volunteer offer lists the check — not in week 1 “just in case” unless your profession requires it.

# Requirements
- Photo ID as listed on the official form
- Payment to the **government or approved channel** shown on that site
- The organisation’s details if they must sight the result

# Step-by-step guide
1. Ask the employer **which** check they need (AFP National Police Check vs state WWCC / Blue Card / Ochre Card). They are different.
2. For a national police check, start at the AFP: [National police checks](https://www.afp.gov.au/national-police-checks).
3. For working with children / vulnerable people, use the **{{state.short}}** portal below.
4. Complete identity steps only on that `.gov.au` (or the official state site listed).
5. Store the PDF/result yourself. We never upload it.

# Common mistakes
- Sending passport photos to a WhatsApp “HR assistant”
- Mixing up a police check with a working with children check
- Paying a random .com site that only forwards you to AFP anyway

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart apply for me?** No, and we should not hold your ID documents.

# Overview
The Great Ocean Road is Victoria’s iconic coastal drive — cliffs, beaches, rainforest, and the Twelve Apostles.

# Why this matters
It is one of the best first big trips from Melbourne: doable as a long day trip or a relaxed overnight.

# When to do it
Best in clear weather. Start early (7–8am) if doing it in one day. Avoid peak holiday weekends if you dislike crowds.

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
Prices change — always check official sites before you go.
- **Fuel (Melbourne return via GOR)**: $80–$140 depending on car and stops
- **Car hire (1 day compact)**: $60–$120 + insurance excess
- **Organised day tour from Melbourne**: $150–$280 pp
- **Twelve Apostles visitor area**: free entry / parking areas apply as signed
- **Otway Fly / paid attractions (optional)**: $30–$55 pp
- **Food & coffee along the way**: $25–$50 pp
- **Overnight (Apollo Bay / Port Campbell motel)**: $160–$280 for 2

# Short itinerary (1 long day)
1. **Depart Melbourne** via Geelong (~1.5–2 hrs to Torquay)
2. Stretch at **Torquay / Bells Beach** lookouts
3. Continue through **Lorne** — short pier / bakery stop
4. Lunch at **Apollo Bay**
5. Arrive **Twelve Apostles** for late-afternoon light
6. Optional: **Loch Ard Gorge** walk (20–40 mins)
7. Return via inland highway if dark (safer than winding coast at night)

# Overnight option (2 days)
**Day 1:** Melbourne → Torquay → Lorne → Apollo Bay (stay)
**Day 2:** Apollo Bay → Twelve Apostles → Loch Ard → return Melbourne

# Stops to explore in between
- **Torquay** — surf culture, Bells Beach lookout
- **Anglesea** — beach + golf links views
- **Lorne** — Erskine Falls detour if time
- **Kennett River** — often good for spotting koalas in trees (keep distance)
- **Apollo Bay** — harbour stroll and fish & chips
- **Great Otway National Park** viewpoints
- **Twelve Apostles & Gibson Steps** area
- **Loch Ard Gorge** — short boardwalks and cliff views

# Common mistakes
- Leaving Melbourne too late and driving the coast in darkness
- Skipping fuel/food stops between towns
- Standing too close to cliff edges in wind

# FAQs
**Can I do it without a car?** Yes — many coach tours from Melbourne.
**Is winter okay?** Yes, but dress warm and expect shorter daylight.

# Useful terms
- **Lookout**: Signed viewing platform
- **National park parks pass**: Some parks elsewhere need passes; check Parks Victoria for current rules

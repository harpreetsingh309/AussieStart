# Overview
If you earn income in Australia you may need to lodge a tax return with the **ATO**. This is a checklist of official pages — AussieStart does not lodge, calculate, or store your tax file.

# Why this matters
Lodging on time (or telling the ATO you don’t need to) avoids extra tax withheld later. Scam texts that “fix your refund” are common.

# When to do it
After the end of the financial year (1 July–30 June), when the ATO says lodgement is open. Keep payslips from your first job onwards.

# Requirements
- TFN given to your employer
- [myGov](https://my.gov.au) linked to the ATO
- Payslips, bank interest summaries, and private health details if asked

# Step-by-step guide
1. Confirm you have a TFN via the [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/tax-file-number).
2. Link the ATO to myGov using only [my.gov.au](https://my.gov.au).
3. Read [Lodge your tax return](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return).
4. Use ATO online services / myTax when the official site says it is available — or a registered tax agent listed on the [Tax Practitioners Board](https://www.tpb.gov.au).
5. Keep records. The ATO explains what to keep: [Records you need to keep](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/income-deductions-offsets-and-records/records-you-need-to-keep).

# Common mistakes
- Clicking an SMS “ATO refund” link (the ATO will not ask you to pay with gift cards)
- Paying an unregistered “agent” on social media
- Forgetting foreign income if you must declare it — read ATO pages, don’t guess

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart file my return?** No.
**Do working holiday makers have different rates?** The ATO publishes working holiday maker rules: [Working holiday makers](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/coming-to-australia-or-going-overseas/coming-to-australia/working-holiday-makers).

# Useful terms
- **myTax**: The ATO’s online lodgement tool inside myGov
- **Notice of assessment**: The ATO’s result after you lodge

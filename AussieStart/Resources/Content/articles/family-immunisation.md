# Overview
Australia has a National Immunisation Program. Schools and childcare often need an up-to-date immunisation history.

# Why this matters
Catch-up vaccines protect your child and are commonly required for enrolment in childcare and school.

# When to do it
Bring overseas records when you arrive. Book a GP catch-up review in the first 1–2 weeks.

# Requirements
- Overseas immunisation records (translated if not in English)
- Medicare / OSHC / private cover details as applicable
- Child’s identity documents

# Step-by-step guide
1. Gather vaccine records from your home country.
2. Book a GP or immunisation clinic appointment.
3. Ask for an Australian Immunisation Register (AIR) catch-up plan.
4. Complete recommended vaccines on schedule.
5. Request an immunisation history statement for school/childcare forms.

# Common mistakes
- Assuming overseas schedules automatically match Australian requirements
- Delaying catch-ups until an enrolment deadline
- Losing paper records — keep digital copies offline

# FAQs
**Is vaccination mandatory?** Requirements vary by state for childcare/school enrolment — many services require an immunisation history statement or approved exemption pathway.
**Where do I get the statement?** Through Medicare online accounts / myGov once recorded on AIR.

# Useful terms
- **AIR**: Australian Immunisation Register
- **Catch-up schedule**: Extra visits to align with Australian recommendations

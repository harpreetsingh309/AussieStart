# Overview
Students and some concession holders can pay less on public transport. You apply with the **state transport agency**, not through AussieStart. Carry the card the agency issues.

# Why this matters
Full adult fares add up quickly. Concession rules and proof (student ID, visa, enrolment) differ by state.

# When to do it
After you have a student ID or enrolment letter, and your state’s transport card or app (Myki, Opal, go card, and so on).

# Requirements
- Proof you are enrolled (student card / e-confirmation)
- Photo ID
- The transport card or account used in {{state.name}}

# Step-by-step guide
1. Buy or open the local transport card/app first (see our free transport guide).
2. Open the **official concession page for {{state.short}}** below — not a reseller.
3. Upload or show whatever that agency lists (student ID, visa, citizenship).
4. Keep a screenshot of approval in case inspectors ask.
5. International students: many states have a **separate** tertiary product. University “student card” alone is not always enough.

# Common mistakes
- Paying a Facebook page to “register concession”
- Assuming a home-country student card works without the state product
- Letting the concession expire after a semester break

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart issue a concession?** No. Only the state transport agency can.
**What about Seniors / Health Care Card?** Those are different products — start at Services Australia: [Concession cards](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/concession-and-health-care-cards).

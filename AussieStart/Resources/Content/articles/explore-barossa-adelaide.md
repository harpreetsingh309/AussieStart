# Overview
From Adelaide you can reach beaches, hills, and the Barossa wine region in under two hours — ideal weekend exploring.

# Why this matters
South Australia rewards food-and-landscape travellers; easy for newcomers based in Adelaide or flying in cheaply.

# When to do it
Autumn harvest vibes are popular; summer is hot — book shaded lunch seats and hydrate.

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
- **Adelaide Metro day fare / metroCARD**: budget $10–$15 city day
- **Barossa day tour**: $150–$220 pp
- **Car hire day**: $60–$100
- **Winery tastings**: $10–$25 pp (often waived with bottle purchase)
- **Lunch at cellar door restaurant**: $40–$80 pp
- **Glenelg tram from city**: standard metro fares
- **Cleland Wildlife Park (optional Hills stop)**: check current entry ~$30 adult range

# Short itinerary (2 days)
**Day 1 — City + coast:** Adelaide CBD → Central Market → Glenelg beach tram afternoon  
**Day 2 — Barossa:** Adelaide → Lyndoch / Tanunda cellar doors → Mengler’s Hill lookout → return

# Stops to explore in between
- **Adelaide Central Market**
- **North Terrace museums** (many free/low cost)
- **Glenelg Jetty**
- **Hahndorf** (Adelaide Hills German heritage town) as alternate day
- **Tanunda / Nuriootpa** Barossa towns
- **Mengler’s Hill Sculpture Park** lookout

# Common mistakes
- Drink-driving after tastings (use a tour or designate a driver)
- Visiting only one big-name winery and missing village walks
- Skipping water and sunscreen in dry heat

# FAQs
**Can kids enjoy Barossa?** Yes — many venues are family-friendly; focus on food, parks, and lookouts.
**Beach and wine in one weekend?** Yes — Glenelg one day, Barossa the next.

# Useful terms
- **Cellar door**: Winery tasting room
- **metroCARD**: Adelaide public transport card

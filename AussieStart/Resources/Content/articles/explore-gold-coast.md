# Overview
The Gold Coast mixes beaches, theme parks, hinterland waterfalls, and easy flights from most Australian capitals.

# Why this matters
Great short break for families and first-time travellers who want beaches + simple logistics.

# When to do it
Year-round. Summer is hotter/more humid; winter is popular for theme parks and clearer beach days.

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
- **Flights (domestic return, sale vs peak)**: $120–$400+ pp
- **Theme park day ticket (e.g. Movie World / Sea World / Wet’n’Wild)**: often $90–$120 online
- **Multi-park passes**: check current bundles
- **Car hire (1–3 days)**: $55–$100/day
- **go card / public transport day**: budget $10–$20 depending on zones
- **Burleigh / Surfers meals**: $20–$45 pp
- **SkyPoint Observation Deck (optional)**: ~$30 adult range
- **Springbrook / hinterland day**: mostly free lookouts + fuel/parking

# Short itinerary (2 days)
**Day 1 — Beach strip:** Surfers Paradise morning → Broadbeach lunch → Burleigh Heads afternoon walk → Mermaid/Narrowneck sunset
**Day 2 — Choice day:** Theme park **or** hinterland (Natural Bridge / Best of All Lookout / Purling Brook if open)

# Stops to explore in between
- **Southport Broadwater**
- **Main Beach / The Spit** (sea wall walk)
- **Kurrawa / Broadbeach** foreshore
- **Burleigh Headland circuit**
- **Currumbin** (wildlife sanctuary optional / beach)
- **Tamborine or Springbrook** hinterland villages and lookouts

# Common mistakes
- Planning outdoor walks in extreme midday heat without water
- Buying theme park tickets at the gate (usually dearer)
- Underestimating parking near Surfers on weekends

# FAQs
**Do I need a car?** Helpful for hinterland; beaches work with tram/bus + rideshare.
**Is it only for parties?** No — Burleigh and hinterland feel calmer than Surfers nightlife.

# Useful terms
- **Hinterland**: Hills and rainforest behind the beach suburbs
- **Patrolled beach**: Swim between the red/yellow flags

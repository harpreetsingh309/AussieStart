# Overview
In a life-threatening emergency anywhere in Australia, call **000**.

# Why this matters
Knowing the right number — and when *not* to call 000 — protects you and keeps emergency lines clear.

# When to do it
Save these numbers on Day 1.

# Requirements
- Working phone with local coverage
- Your address written somewhere easy to find

# Step-by-step guide
1. Save **000** and know it works from mobile and landline.
2. Save poisons information, SES (floods/storms), and your roadside assist if you drive.
3. Learn the address of your home and workplace in clear English.
4. For non-urgent medical advice, consider healthdirect before heading to ED.

# Common mistakes
- Calling 000 for non-emergencies
- Not knowing your street address during a stressful call
- Ignoring bushfire / flood warnings in your area

# FAQs
**Is 911 used here?** No — use **000**.
**What about Triple Zero text?** Available in limited accessibility scenarios — prefer a voice call when you can.

# Useful terms
- **SES**: State Emergency Service
- **ED**: Hospital Emergency Department

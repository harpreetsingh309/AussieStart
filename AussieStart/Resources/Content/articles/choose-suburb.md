# Overview
Picking a suburb is a personal trade-off: rent, commute, schools, and hazards. AussieStart does not rank suburbs or sell listings. Use official maps and your state’s tenancy pages before you pay a bond.

# Why this matters
A cheap listing far from work can cost more in fares. Flood, bushfire, and school-zone rules are published by government, not by Facebook groups.

# When to do it
Before you sign a lease — ideally while you still have a short-stay backup.

# Requirements
- Your likely work/campus postcode
- Budget (see first-month money checklist)
- Time to check official hazard and school catchments

# Step-by-step guide
1. Shortlist 2–3 suburbs using commute time on the state’s transport site (linked below).
2. Check **school catchments** if you have children (education department finder for {{state.short}}).
3. Check flood / fire / storm information via Geoscience Australia and your SES.
4. Confirm tenancy rules on the official renting page for {{state.name}} before you inspect.
5. Never pay a holding deposit to a personal account. See rental bond and scams guides.

- Choosing only from Instagram “best suburb” lists
- Skipping flood overlays in inner-city pockets
- Signing after one video tour with no condition report plan

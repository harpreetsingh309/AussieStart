# Overview
Groceries, furniture, and phones are easy to find — but prices and return rules differ from home.

# Why this matters
Smart shopping in week 1 reduces waste and protects your bond when renting furnished vs unfurnished.

# When to do it
After you have a SIM, transport, and a place to store purchases.

# Requirements
- Debit/credit card
- Delivery address
- Measuring tape for furniture

# Step-by-step guide
1. Start with Aldi / Coles / Woolworths for groceries; Indian/Asian grocers for familiar staples.
2. For furniture, compare Marketplace, IKEA, and second-hand hubs.
3. Keep receipts for returns (usually 14–30 days — check the store).
4. Beware marketplace scams — never pay deposit without seeing the item.
5. Buy only powerboards / adapters that meet Australian standards.

# Common mistakes
- Overbuying furniture before confirming lease length
- Using unsafe travel adapters
- Ignoring unit by-laws about BBQ / drying clothes

# FAQs
**What voltage is used?** Generally 230V with Australian plugs (Type I).

# Useful terms
- **Click & Collect**: Order online, pick up in store
- **GST**: Goods and Services Tax included in most retail prices

# Overview
Finding work in Australia usually means a local-style resume, Seek/Indeed/LinkedIn applications, and knowing Fair Work basics.

# Why this matters
Work rights depend on your visa — and underpayment is common when people don’t know minimum standards.

# When to do it
Students: after settling SIM/bank/TFN. Skilled migrants: begin networking early, apply once TFN/bank are ready.

# Requirements
- Visa work rights confirmed
- TFN and bank account
- Updated resume (2 pages max is common)
- References or LinkedIn recommendations

# Step-by-step guide
1. Confirm hours/conditions allowed on your visa.
2. Rewrite your resume for Australian recruiters.
3. Set job alerts on Seek, Indeed, and LinkedIn.
4. Prepare for behavioural interviews (STAR method).
5. Check every payslip against hours and casual loadings.

# Common mistakes
- Working beyond visa hour limits
- Accepting cash-in-hand without payslips
- Not reading Fair Work award rates

# FAQs
**What is Fair Work?** The national workplace relations system setting minimum entitlements.
**Do I need an ABN to be employed?** No — employees use TFN; ABN is for contracting/business.

# Useful terms
- **Award**: Minimum pay/conditions instrument for an industry
- **Casual loading**: Extra % pay instead of leave entitlements

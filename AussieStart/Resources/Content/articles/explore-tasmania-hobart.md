# Overview
Hobart is the friendliest gateway to Tasmania — harbour city charm, MONA, and day trips to wilderness edges.

# Why this matters
Ideal long weekend if you want cooler climate, food culture, and nature without a huge road itinerary.

# When to do it
Summer for longer days; winter for Aurora chances and cosy food scenes (pack warm clothes).

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
- **Flights to Hobart (return)**: $180–$500+ pp
- **MONA entry**: often ~$40–$50 adult (Tasmanians sometimes free/discounted — check)
- **MONA ferry from Hobart**: ~$30–$50 return range
- **kunanyi / Mount Wellington return shuttle or fuel**: $20–$60
- **Port Arthur day trip (entry)**: ~$50 adult range + transport
- **Bruny Island tour**: $150–$280 pp
- **Meals in Salamanca / CBD**: $25–$50 pp

# Short itinerary (3 days)
**Day 1:** Salamanca / waterfront → Battery Point walk → Signal Station views  
**Day 2:** MONA ferry day  
**Day 3:** kunanyi/Mount Wellington morning **or** Bruny/Port Arthur long day

# Stops to explore in between
- **Salamanca Place**
- **Battery Point cottages**
- **Tasman Bridge lookout areas**
- **Richmond** (if driving) — historic bridge town on Port Arthur route
- **Kettering** ferry terminal (for Bruny)
- **Mountain lookouts** on kunanyi road (weather dependent)

# Common mistakes
- Not checking mountain weather (road can close)
- Booking MONA ferry only and missing timed entry needs on busy days
- Overpacking a single day with Port Arthur + Bruny

# FAQs
**Is a car essential?** Helpful, but MONA + Hobart walkables work with transfers.
**How many days minimum?** 2–3 full days feels right.

# Useful terms
- **kunanyi**: Contempory dual name used with Mount Wellington
- **MONA**: Museum of Old and New Art

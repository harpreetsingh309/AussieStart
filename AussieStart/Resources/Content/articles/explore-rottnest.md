# Overview
Rottnest Island (Wadjemup) near Perth is famous for turquoise bays, bikes, and quokkas — an easy WA classic.

# Why this matters
A simple ferry day trip that feels like a proper holiday without leaving Western Australia.

# When to do it
Summer packs ferries and bikes — book ahead. Shoulder seasons are lovely and quieter.

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
- **Ferry Fremantle / Perth return**: often $60–$90 adult range
- **Bike hire**: $30–$45/day
- **Snorkel gear hire**: $15–$30
- **Museum / attractions (optional)**: $10–$20
- **Lunch on island**: $20–$40 pp (limited options — prices higher than mainland)
- **Segway / tours (optional)**: $50–$90
- **Accommodation overnight**: $200–$400+ depending on season

# Short itinerary (1 day)
1. Morning ferry from **Fremantle**
2. Collect bikes → **The Basin** snorkel/swim
3. Ride toward **Parker Point / Pinky Beach** area
4. Lunch near Thomson Bay settlement
5. Quokka-friendly paths (do not touch/feed)
6. Afternoon ferry back

# Stops to explore in between
- **Fremantle** — arrive early for coffee/markets if time
- **Thomson Bay** settlement
- **The Basin**
- **Little Salmon Bay**
- **Parker Point**
- **Lighthouse / West End** if you are a strong cyclist with time

# Common mistakes
- Missing the last ferry
- Touching quokkas (harmful and not allowed)
- Underestimating sun exposure while cycling

# FAQs
**Do I need a car on Rottnest?** No — bikes, buses, and walking cover visitor areas.
**Can I bring food?** Yes — many visitors pack snacks; check ferry rules.

# Useful terms
- **Wadjemup**: Traditional name for Rottnest
- **Quokka**: Small local marsupial

# Overview
Once you have a lease (or a share-house agreement), you usually set up electricity, and sometimes gas and internet, in your name. AussieStart does not sign you up with a retailer.

# Why this matters
Moving in without power or internet makes everything else harder — job applications, TFN codes, and maps included.

# When to do it
As soon as your lease start date is confirmed. Some retailers need a few days to connect.

# Requirements
- Address and move-in date
- Photo ID
- Bank account or card for the connection fee / usage
- Permission from your head tenant if you are in a share house (bills may already be in their name)

# Step-by-step guide
1. Ask the agent or current tenant whether electricity/gas/internet are already connected and whose name is on the bill.
2. Compare retailers using official comparison sites where they exist, then sign up **on the retailer’s own website**.
3. Take meter photos on move-in day.
4. For internet, check building type (NBN) with the official NBN checker, then pick a plan you can afford to exit.
5. Put reminder dates in your phone for the first bill so usage does not surprise you.

# Common mistakes
- Paying a stranger on social media to “set up NBN”
- Not photographing the meter (disputes later)
- Signing a long internet contract before you know if you will move

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart connect my power?** No. You (or your housemate) deal with the retailer.
**Is the landlord always responsible?** For most private rentals, tenants arrange electricity. Always read your lease.

# Useful terms
- **NBN**: The wholesale network most home internet plans use
- **Connection fee**: A one-off retailer charge to start supply

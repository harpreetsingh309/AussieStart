# Overview
Driving rules and licence conversion differ by state. In {{state.name}}, confirm whether you can drive on your overseas licence and for how long.

# Why this matters
Driving without a valid licence or correct insurance can mean fines and claim refusals.

# When to do it
Before buying a car — usually after weeks 2–4 once other basics are done.

# Requirements
- Current overseas licence (and official translation if not in English)
- Passport / visa
- Address evidence
- Eyesight check for new licences

# Step-by-step guide
1. Read {{state.short}} road rules (hook turns, school zones, tolls).
2. Check how long your overseas licence is valid after arrival.
3. Book any required tests early — wait times vary.
4. Arrange compulsory third party (CTP) / comprehensive insurance before driving.
5. Learn toll road tag/account requirements in your city.

# Common mistakes
- Assuming international driving permits replace a licence forever
- Ignoring toll invoices until they escalate
- Buying a car without a proper PPSR check

# FAQs
**Do I need a local licence immediately?** It depends on your state and visa/residency — verify with the state roads authority.

# Useful terms
- **CTP**: Compulsory third party personal injury insurance
- **PPSR**: Register to check if a used car has finance/writs

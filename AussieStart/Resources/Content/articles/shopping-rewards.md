# Overview
Australia's two big supermarket groups each run a free points program — Flybuys at Coles and Everyday Rewards at Woolworths. Joining both costs nothing and is the easiest way to stop overpaying on groceries in your first months.

# Why this matters
Groceries are one of the largest controllable costs in a new household budget. The savings come less from the points themselves than from the member-only prices and the weekly specials cycle. Shoppers who ignore both routinely pay noticeably more for exactly the same basket.

Points are worth roughly half a cent each: 2,000 points converts to $10 off in either program. Earning at the base rate of 1 point per dollar, a typical household collects somewhere around $40–60 a year. Useful, but the specials are where the real money is.

# When to do it
Sign up in your first week, before your first big shop. Both take a few minutes and need no Australian credit history, no visa evidence and no fee.

# Requirements
- An email address and an Australian mobile number
- A residential address
- The Flybuys and Everyday Rewards apps, or a physical card from the service desk

# Step-by-step guide
1. **Join Flybuys** at flybuys.com.au or in the Flybuys app. Link it to Coles, and also to Kmart, Target, Bunnings and Officeworks if you shop there.
2. **Join Everyday Rewards** at everyday.com.au or in the app. It covers Woolworths, BIG W, BWS and Ampol service stations.
3. **Scan every single time.** Member-only prices are not applied unless your card or app barcode is scanned before payment. This is the biggest saving and it has nothing to do with points.
4. **Activate your weekly offers in the app** before shopping. Both programs push personalised bonus-point offers that only count if you tap to activate them first.
5. **Learn the specials cycle.** New catalogue specials start each Wednesday at both chains and run for one week. Many pantry and household items rotate on a predictable half-price cycle, so if something you use is half price, buy enough to last until it comes around again.
6. **Redeem when it suits you.** At Coles you can now pay with points at the register in set amounts from $10 up to $100, depending on your balance — this replaced the old $10-only limit in mid-2026. Woolworths redeems in $10 blocks, or you can "bank for Christmas" and take the lot in December.
7. **Consider the airline conversion** only if you actually fly. Flybuys converts to Velocity (Virgin) and Everyday Rewards to Qantas Points. Converting is not automatically better value than taking money off your shop — compare before you commit.

# Common mistakes
- Forgetting to scan, then wondering why the shelf price and the register price differ
- Not activating boost offers, which then never apply
- Chasing points on items you would not otherwise buy — a "bonus points" product at full price is usually worse value than the half-price alternative
- Signing up with a temporary overseas phone number you will lose
- Assuming the Coles and Woolworths apps show the same price as the shelf in every store — check the shelf label, which is what the register uses

# FAQs
**Do points expire?** Both programs can expire points after a long period of account inactivity. Scanning occasionally is enough to keep an account active.

**Can I join before I have a permanent address?** Yes. You can update your address later, and neither program requires proof of residency.

**Is Aldi part of a points program?** No. Aldi runs no loyalty scheme and instead prices low across the board. Many households shop Aldi for staples and use Coles or Woolworths for specials.

**Do I need the credit card versions?** No. The free membership gives you the member prices and the base earn rate. The co-branded credit cards are separate financial products with their own fees and conditions.

**Are the apps collecting my shopping data?** Yes — that is the trade. Both programs record what you buy in order to personalise offers. Each app has privacy settings that let you limit some data use.

# Useful terms
- **Member price / Everyday Extra price**: A discount shown only to scanned members
- **Boost / activate**: Tapping an offer in the app so it counts at the register
- **Catalogue**: The weekly specials leaflet, published online each Wednesday
- **Pay with points**: Redeeming points as money off at the checkout
- **Half-price cycle**: The predictable rotation of deep discounts on staple items

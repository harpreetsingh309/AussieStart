# Overview
Centrelink payments (if you are eligible) are claimed through **Services Australia** and **myGov**. AussieStart cannot submit claims, see your CRN, or tell you that you will be paid. International students are often ineligible — always check the official finder.

# Why this matters
Fake “Centrelink refund” texts are common. The real start is [servicesaustralia.gov.au](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au) and [my.gov.au](https://my.gov.au).

# When to do it
After you have a local address, phone, and (usually) myGov, and only if a payment on the official finder matches your visa/residency.

# Requirements
- Passport / visa evidence
- TFN if Services Australia asks
- Bank account in your name
- Documents listed on that payment’s official page

# Step-by-step guide
1. Use the official [Payment and service finder](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/payment-and-service-finder) — answer the questions yourself.
2. Read the **eligibility** page for that payment on servicesaustralia.gov.au (do not stop at a blog summary).
3. Sign in to [myGov](https://my.gov.au) and link Centrelink when the official site instructs you to.
4. Claim only inside those sites, or at a [service centre](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/service-centres) if they require it.
5. If you are a family visa holder, also see our free Centrelink basics guide. Students: confirm visa rules on [Home Affairs](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au) before you assume a payment exists.

# Common mistakes
- Paying someone to “submit Centrelink”
- Using a .com clone of myGov
- Assuming every new arrival gets Family Tax Benefit

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart see if I’ll get a payment?** No.
**What is a CRN?** Customer Reference Number issued by Services Australia — only they create it.

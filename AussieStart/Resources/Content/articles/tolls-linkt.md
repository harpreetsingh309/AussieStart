# Overview
Many motorways in {{state.name}} are **toll roads**. You pay with a tag or a video account (often branded **Linkt** or similar). AussieStart does not open accounts or pay invoices. Set this up **before** you drive, especially in a rental car.

# Why this matters
Unpaid tolls become reminder fees, then debt collection. A tag left in a used car can bill the previous owner — or you.

# When to do it
Before you first drive on a motorway in {{state.short}}, or the same day you collect a hire car.

# Requirements
- Number plate of the car you will drive
- Credit/debit card
- Licence and Australian mobile if the operator asks

# Step-by-step guide
1. Confirm whether your trip uses a toll road (maps often show “toll”). If you are hiring a car, read the **rental desk** toll product — they may charge a daily fee and pass on tolls.
2. Open an account with the operator that covers {{state.short}} **before** you drive, or follow the “pay later / number plate” option they list (time limits apply).
3. Match the plate on the account to the car. Remove old tags when you sell or return a car.
4. Turn on email/SMS invoices. Pay on the operator’s real website — not a link in a random text.
5. If you get a notice, pay by the due date or dispute using the details **on that notice**.

## Victoria
Melbourne tollways overview (VicRoads): [Melbourne tollways](https://www.vicroads.vic.gov.au/traffic-and-road-use/road-network-and-performance/melbourne-tollways).  
CityLink / EastLink accounts are run by operators (often Linkt). Start from the VicRoads page, then complete signup on the operator site listed there.

## NSW
NSW toll roads: [Toll roads (NSW)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/roads-safety-and-rules/toll-roads).  
Service NSW also explains tolls: [service.nsw.gov.au](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au). Sydney motorways often use Linkt or WestConnex accounts — use the NSW page, not a sponsored ad.

## Queensland
TMR toll information: [Tolls (Queensland)](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/projects/tolls).  
Brisbane / Gold Coast motorways have operator accounts (including Linkt in some corridors). Follow TMR, then the operator named on that page.

<!-- state:sa -->
## South Australia
Most Adelaide driving is not a Melbourne-style motorway tag network. Check [sa.gov.au driving](https://www.sa.gov.au/topics/driving-and-transport) before you assume you need a tag. Airport or commercial car-park fees are separate.
<!-- /state -->

<!-- state:wa -->
## Western Australia
Perth does not use the same east-coast Linkt motorway model. Check [wa.gov.au / Transport](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/department-of-transport) for tunnels and any new charging. Airport parking is not a state toll tag.
<!-- /state -->

<!-- state:tas -->
## Tasmania
No Melbourne-style urban toll motorway network. Still pay any **bridge or ferry** fees listed by the operator on official TAS pages: [tas.gov.au](https://www.tas.gov.au).
<!-- /state -->

<!-- state:act -->
## ACT
Canberra does not run an east-coast Linkt-style motorway tag as a daily necessity. If you drive to Sydney, set up a **NSW** toll account before you leave. Access Canberra: [accesscanberra.act.gov.au](https://www.accesscanberra.act.gov.au).
<!-- /state -->

<!-- state:nt -->
## Northern Territory
No east-coast style urban toll tag network. Check [nt.gov.au](https://nt.gov.au) for road conditions. Darwin airport parking is not Linkt.
<!-- /state -->

# Common mistakes
- Driving a hire car “for a day” with no toll product
- Ignoring the first invoice until extra fees apply
- Paying a lookalike “toll fine” website
- Leaving the previous owner’s tag in a used car

# FAQs
**Is Linkt the government?** No. It is a commercial operator. Use `.gov.au` pages to understand the roads, then the operator named there to pay.
**Can AussieStart pay my toll?** No.

# Useful terms
- **Tag**: Electronic transponder in the windscreen
- **Video matching**: Camera reads the plate if there is no tag

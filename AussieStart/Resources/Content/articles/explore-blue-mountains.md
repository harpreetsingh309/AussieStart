# Overview
The Blue Mountains are a classic day trip from Sydney — escarpment views, villages, and bushwalks west of the city.

# Why this matters
Easy public-transport access, big views, and a gentle introduction to Australian national parks.

# When to do it
Aim for a weekday or early weekend start. Fog can hide views in mornings — afternoons are often clearer.

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
- **Train Sydney ↔ Katoomba (Opal / adult return zone-style)**: often ~$10–$20 with Opal caps/discounts varying — check Transport NSW
- **Scenic World rides (optional)**: ~$50–$60 adult day pass range
- **Echo Point / Three Sisters lookouts**: free
- **National park vehicle day pass (if driving)**: check NSW National Parks current fees (~$8–$12 class vehicles often)
- **Lunch in Katoomba / Leura**: $20–$40 pp
- **Guided day tour from Sydney**: $140–$250 pp

# Short itinerary (1 day by train)
1. Morning train to **Katoomba**
2. Walk or bus to **Echo Point** (Three Sisters)
3. Short **Prince Henry Cliff Walk** section if energy allows
4. Lunch in **Katoomba** or prettier **Leura** village
5. Optional: **Scenic World** or **Wentworth Falls** lookout
6. Evening train back to Sydney

# Stops to explore in between
- **Strathfield / Parramatta** — only if combining errands; otherwise stay on the Mountains train
- **Glenbrook** — gateway town if driving
- **Wentworth Falls** — waterfall lookouts
- **Leura** — cafes, boutiques, cliff walks
- **Katoomba** — Echo Point, town centre
- **Blackheath** (if you have a car/extra time) — Govetts Leap lookout

# Common mistakes
- Underestimating walking time between station and Echo Point
- Visiting only Scenic World and missing free lookouts
- Wearing city shoes on wet boardwalks

# FAQs
**Do I need a car?** No — train + local buses/walking works well.
**Is it cold?** Often cooler than Sydney — bring a layer.

# Useful terms
- **Escarpment**: Steep cliff edge of the plateau
- **Boardwalk**: Built walking path for safety

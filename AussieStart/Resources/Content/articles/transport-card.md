# Overview
Public transport in {{state.name}} uses **{{state.transport}}**.

# Why this matters
Buses, trains, and trams are cheaper and simpler once your card or account is set up.

# When to do it
Day 1–4, before your first regular commute.

# Requirements
- Smartphone for the official transport app / digital card where available
- Or a physical card from a station / retail outlet
- Payment method to top up

# Step-by-step guide
1. Download the official {{state.short}} transport app if offered.
2. Create {{state.transport}} / account and verify your details.
3. Top up credit or buy a pass that matches your travel pattern.
4. Always tap on / tap off where required.
5. Keep a small backup balance for peak travel weeks.

# Common mistakes
- Not tapping off and paying a default fare
- Letting credit hit zero mid-journey
- Assuming airport services always use the same card rules

# FAQs
**Can I pay with credit card on every bus?** Not always — {{state.transport}} is the reliable default.

# Useful terms
- **Tap on / tap off**: Start and end of a journey validation
- **Concession**: Reduced fare if eligible

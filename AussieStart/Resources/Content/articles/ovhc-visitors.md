# Overview
**OVHC** (Overseas Visitor Health Cover) is private health insurance some **visitor and temporary visas** require. It is not Medicare and not OSHC (students). AussieStart cannot sell or activate a policy.

# Why this matters
A GP or hospital visit without cover can mean a large bill. Some visa grants say you must hold OVHC for the whole stay. Lookalike “insurance agents” on WhatsApp are a common scam.

# When to do it
Before you travel, or as soon as your grant letter lists health-cover conditions. If you are a student, use the OSHC guide instead.

# Requirements
- Passport and visa subclass (for example 600, 482, 485 — check **your** grant)
- Credit card for the insurer’s official site
- Time to read what is excluded (pregnancy, dental, pre-existing)

# Step-by-step guide
1. Read your visa conditions on [VEVO](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/already-have-a-visa/check-visa-details-and-conditions/check-conditions-online) or the grant letter — not a Facebook post.
2. Read the government OVHC page: [Overseas Visitor Health Cover](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/overseas/overseas_visitors_health_cover.htm).
3. Buy only from an insurer listed for OVHC / the provider named in your visa documents. Complete payment on **their** website.
4. Save the policy PDF and membership number offline.
5. If Medicare eligibility applies later (some work visas), read [Enrolling in Medicare](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/enrolling-medicare) — do not assume OVHC and Medicare are the same.

# Common mistakes
- Buying OSHC when your visa asks for OVHC (or the reverse)
- Letting cover lapse while still in Australia
- Paying a “priority certificate” to someone who messaged you first

# FAQs
**Is OVHC the same as travel insurance?** Usually no. Travel insurance is for trips; OVHC is the visa-style hospital/medical product described on privatehealth.gov.au.
**Can AussieStart be my insurer?** No.

# Useful terms
- **OVHC**: Overseas Visitor Health Cover
- **OSHC**: Overseas Student Health Cover (student visas)
- **VEVO**: Official visa-conditions check

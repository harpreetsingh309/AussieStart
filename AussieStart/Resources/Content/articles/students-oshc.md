# Overview
Most international students must keep Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the visa period.

# Why this matters
OSHC helps with medically necessary treatment and is a visa condition for many student visas.

# When to do it
Arrange before arrival; confirm policy details in week 1.

# Requirements
- Student visa details
- OSHC policy number
- University enrolment evidence

# Step-by-step guide
1. Confirm your OSHC provider and membership number.
2. Learn what is covered vs excluded (optics, dental often limited).
3. Find GPs that accept your OSHC claiming process.
4. Keep digital copies of your policy in offline files.
5. Renew before expiry — gaps can affect visa compliance.

# Common mistakes
- Letting cover lapse
- Assuming OSHC equals full Medicare
- Visiting ED for minor issues without checking urgent care options

# FAQs
**Is OSHC the same as Medicare?** No. Some students later become Medicare-eligible; many rely on OSHC.

# Useful terms
- **OSHC**: Overseas Student Health Cover
- **Gap fee**: Amount you pay above what insurance covers

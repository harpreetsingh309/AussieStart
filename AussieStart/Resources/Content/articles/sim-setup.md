# Overview
A local Australian SIM (or eSIM) gives you a local number for banks, deliveries, TFN, and job applications.

# Why this matters
Many services send SMS codes. Without a local number, account setup gets stuck.

# When to do it
Day 1 — ideally at the airport or the same afternoon you land.

# Requirements
- Passport for prepaid ID checks (rules vary by provider)
- Unlocked phone (or dual-SIM / eSIM capable)
- Payment method

# Step-by-step guide
1. Choose prepaid first unless your employer provides a plan.
2. Compare Optus, Telstra, Vodafone, and MVNOs for coverage where you live.
3. Buy a physical SIM or activate an eSIM.
4. Install, restart, and enable mobile data.
5. Save your new number and update banking / university contacts.

# Common mistakes
- Buying a plan with poor coverage in your suburb
- Forgetting to turn off expensive international roaming
- Not keeping the packaging until activation succeeds

# FAQs
**Prepaid or postpaid?** Prepaid is simpler for new arrivals.
**Do I need a TFN for a SIM?** No.

# Useful terms
- **MVNO**: Smaller brands that use a major network
- **eSIM**: Digital SIM built into newer phones

# Overview
Childcare in Australia includes long day care, family day care, outside-school-hours care, and occasional care.

# Why this matters
Reliable childcare lets parents work or study — and quality services support early learning.

# When to do it
Start researching before you arrive if possible. Popular centres can have waitlists.

# Requirements
- Child’s details and immunisation status
- Parent ID and contact details
- CRN (Customer Reference Number) if claiming Child Care Subsidy
- Work/study details for subsidy assessment

# Step-by-step guide
1. Search centres near home or work on StartingBlocks.gov.au.
2. Compare fees, hours, ratios, and cultural/language fit.
3. Tour 2–3 centres and ask about vacancies and settling-in.
4. Register interest / join the waitlist.
5. If eligible, claim **Child Care Subsidy** via Centrelink / myGov.
6. Confirm enrolment contract, bonds, and notice periods in writing.

# Common mistakes
- Signing up without checking real daily fees after subsidy
- Ignoring waitlist timelines for popular suburbs
- Not updating Centrelink when work hours change

# FAQs
**What is CCS?** Child Care Subsidy — a government contribution toward approved childcare fees for eligible families.
**Do I need a TFN for childcare?** Parents usually need Centrelink / myGov setup; TFN helps with broader government services.

# Useful terms
- **Long day care**: Centre-based care, often for working parents
- **OSHC**: Outside School Hours Care (before/after school)

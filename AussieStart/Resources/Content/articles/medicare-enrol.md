# Overview
Medicare is Australia’s public health insurance. This guide explains **how people usually enrol on official websites**. AussieStart cannot enrol you, see your records, or speak to Services Australia for you.

# Why this matters
Without Medicare (or OSHC if you are an international student), GP and hospital bills can be large. Enrolment is done only through Services Australia.

# When to do it
After you have a local address and phone, and you believe you are eligible (for example many permanent visas, some work visas, or reciprocal health-care agreements). International students: start with your [OSHC](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/overseas/overseas_student_health_cover.htm) insurer, not Medicare, unless Services Australia says you are eligible.

# Requirements
- Passport and visa grant notice
- Proof of address in Australia
- Details to create or use [myGov](https://my.gov.au)
- Time to complete identity checks on **.gov.au** sites only

# Step-by-step guide
1. Read eligibility on Services Australia: [Enrolling in Medicare](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/enrolling-medicare).
2. Create or sign in to [myGov](https://my.gov.au), then link Medicare when the official site offers that step.
3. If the website asks you to visit a service centre, book or walk in using [Find a service centre](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/service-centres). Take original ID.
4. After you have a Medicare card, find a GP and ask about bulk billing. See our free Medicare & GP guide.
5. For after-hours non-emergencies use [healthdirect](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au) or call **1800 022 222**. Life-threatening: **000**.

# Common mistakes
- Using a lookalike “Medicare login” that is not `.gov.au`
- Assuming every visa includes Medicare (many temporary visas do not)
- Skipping OSHC if your student visa requires it

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart submit my enrolment?** No. Only you use [servicesaustralia.gov.au](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au).
**Is this medical advice?** No. It is a map to official pages.

# Useful terms
- **Bulk billing**: The clinic accepts the Medicare benefit as full payment
- **OSHC**: Overseas Student Health Cover, often required by student visas

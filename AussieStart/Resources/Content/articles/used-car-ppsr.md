# Overview
Before you buy a used car, check finance and stolen-vehicle records on the **PPSR** (government register) and confirm licence and roadworthy rules for {{state.short}}. AussieStart does not sell cars or run PPSR searches for you.

# Why this matters
A cheap car with unpaid finance can be repossessed. Unregistered or uninsured driving can mean fines and no cover after a crash.

# When to do it
After you know you may drive in {{state.name}}, and **before** you transfer money.

# Requirements
- VIN / chassis number from the vehicle
- Your overseas or local licence status (see driving guide)
- Card payment for the official PPSR search fee

# Step-by-step guide
1. Confirm you are allowed to drive: use your state roads authority (links below).
2. Search the vehicle on the official PPSR: [ppsr.gov.au](https://www.ppsr.gov.au).
3. Ask for a receipt in the seller’s name and match it to the VIN on the car.
4. Arrange **CTP** (compulsory third party) as required in {{state.short}} before you drive away.
5. Budget stamp duty / transfer on the state transport site — amounts change, so read the page, don’t use old blogs.

# Common mistakes
- Using a third-party “VIN check” instead of [ppsr.gov.au](https://www.ppsr.gov.au)
- Driving uninsured between purchase and CTP start
- Ignoring toll tags if the car is already linked to someone else’s account

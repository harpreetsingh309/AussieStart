# Overview
Private hospital insurance is optional for most people. It is **not** Medicare. AussieStart does not sell policies. Use [privatehealth.gov.au](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au) and [Moneysmart](https://moneysmart.gov.au/private-health-insurance) to understand waiting periods before you buy.

# Why this matters
Insurers can make you wait before they pay for some treatments. If you buy cover today, pregnancy and some extras are often **not** covered immediately. Some taxpayers also pay a **Medicare levy surcharge** if they do not hold an appropriate hospital policy.

# When to do it
After you know you will stay in Australia and you are considering hospital cover — not in week 1 unless a tax or visa adviser has told you to. Students: start with OSHC, not this product.

# Requirements
- Medicare number if you have one
- Time to read hospital vs extras vs ambulance
- Income estimate if you are checking the surcharge (ATO)

# Step-by-step guide
1. Read how private health works: [How it works (privatehealth.gov.au)](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/how_it_works/index.htm).
2. Read waiting periods: [Waiting periods](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/how_it_works/waiting_periods.htm). Typical rules many funds use: **2 months** for many extras and some hospital; **12 months** for pre-existing conditions and obstetric (pregnancy) hospital treatment. Confirm on the fund’s own PDS.
3. Check whether you might pay the Medicare levy surcharge: [ATO — Medicare levy surcharge](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/medicare-and-private-health-insurance/medicare-levy-surcharge).
4. Compare using the government site, then open the **insurer’s own** quote page. Ignore SMS “renewal” links.
5. If you already had an equivalent Australian hospital policy, ask about **waiting-period waivers** when switching. Lifetime Health Cover loading is separate: [Lifetime Health Cover](https://www.privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/how_it_works/lifetime_health_cover.htm).

# Common mistakes
- Thinking private health replaces Medicare GP bulk billing
- Booking elective surgery during a waiting period
- Buying extras-only and assuming hospital is included
- Using a lookalike “compare” site that is not `.gov.au`

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart take out a policy?** No.
**Are waiting periods the same at every fund?** No. The government page explains the usual maximums; the Product Disclosure Statement is the contract.
**Is this tax advice?** No. Use the ATO pages or a registered agent.

# Useful terms
- **Hospital cover**: Private hospital / day surgery (not GP visits)
- **Extras**: Dental, optical, physio — usually separate
- **PDS**: Product Disclosure Statement

# Overview
A **condition report** is the room-by-room record you and the agent complete at move-in. Photos plus that form protect your bond. AussieStart does not lodge reports or bonds.

# Why this matters
Without dated photos, “pre-existing damage” disputes are hard. Most states expect you to return the report within a short deadline printed on the form.

# When to do it
On move-in day (or the day you collect keys), before you unpack.

# Requirements
- The official condition report form for {{state.name}} (from the tenancy agency site below)
- Phone camera with date/time enabled
- Torch for ovens, wardrobes, and ceilings

# Step-by-step guide
1. Download the **current** condition report from your state tenancy site (links below) — not a random blog PDF.
2. Walk every room: walls, floors, blinds, stove, bathroom silicone, flyscreens, remote controls.
3. Photograph meter readings and any existing mould, chips, or stains.
4. Return the signed report by the deadline on the form.
5. Keep your own copy + photos in two places (phone and email).

# Common mistakes
- Writing “all good” without photos
- Missing the return deadline
- Paying bond to a personal account instead of the state authority

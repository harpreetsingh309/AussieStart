# Overview
Sydney’s harbour icons — Opera House, Bridge, ferry hops, and foreshore walks — make a perfect first weekend in NSW.

# Why this matters
You can cover a lot using Opal ferries and walking, without hiring a car.

# When to do it
Clear days are best for photos. Book Opera House tours ahead in peak season.

# Typical fees (AUD, approx.)
- **Opera House guided tour**: ~$45–$50 adult (tour types vary)
- **BridgeClimb (optional premium)**: ~$300–$450+ adult
- **Pylon Lookout**: ~$20 adult range
- **Ferry rides (Opal)**: standard ferry fares / daily caps apply
- **Manly ferry return vibe + beach time**: budget $15–$30 transport + food
- **Lunch at Circular Quay / The Rocks**: $25–$50 pp
- **Taronga Zoo ferry + entry (optional)**: zoo tickets often $50–$60 adult + ferry

# Short itinerary (1 day)
1. Morning photos at **Opera House** / Circular Quay
2. Walk to **The Rocks** laneways
3. Ferry to **Milsons Point** for Lavender Bay / Luna Park views (or climb Bridge Pylon)
4. Lunch in **Kirribilli / Milsons Point** area or back at Quay
5. Afternoon ferry to **Manly** — Corso + beach stroll
6. Sunset return ferry to Circular Quay

# Stops to explore in between
- **Royal Botanic Garden** edge paths
- **Mrs Macquarie’s Chair** lookout
- **The Rocks Markets** (weekend)
- **Barangaroo Reserve**
- **Balls Head / Blues Point** lookouts (North Sydney side)
- **Manly** — Shelly Beach walk if time

# Common mistakes
- Trying BridgeClimb + Zoo + Manly in one rushed day
- Ignoring ferry wharf queues at peak times
- Leaving Opera House tour booking too late

# FAQs
**Is the Opera House exterior free?** Yes — walking around outside is free; interior tours are paid.
**Best free view?** Mrs Macquarie’s Chair or Blues Point Reserve.

# Useful terms
- **Wharf**: Ferry terminal
- **Opal**: NSW transport smartcard / app account

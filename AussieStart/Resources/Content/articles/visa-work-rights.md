# Overview
Your visa — not your job offer — decides whether you can work, and for how many hours. This guide explains how to **check** rights on official tools. AussieStart cannot view, change, or extend visas.

# Why this matters
Working more hours than your visa allows can affect your stay. Employers also need to confirm rights before they roster you.

# When to do it
Before you accept paid work, and again if your visa grant changes.

# Requirements
- Passport
- Visa grant notice or visa details
- Access to the official VEVO service (Visa Entitlement Verification Online)

# Step-by-step guide
1. Open **VEVO** on the Department of Home Affairs website (confirm it is `.gov.au`).
2. Check work conditions in plain language: permitted hours, employer limits, and study requirements if any.
3. Save or screenshot the result for your own records. Share it with an employer only if you choose to.
4. If something looks wrong, contact Home Affairs through official channels — not social media “agents”.
5. International students: track fortnightly hours yourself; public holidays do not always reset a limit.

# Common mistakes
- Trusting a Facebook post instead of VEVO
- Starting work on a bridging visa without reading the new conditions
- Paying someone who promises a “faster visa” outside official processes

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart run VEVO for me?** No. Only you (or an employer you authorise) should use the official tool.
**Is this immigration advice?** No. It is a checklist so you know where to look.

# Useful terms
- **VEVO**: Official tool to see current visa conditions
- **Work limitation**: Hour cap or “no work” condition on a visa

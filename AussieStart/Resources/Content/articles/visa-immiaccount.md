# Overview
Visa applications, extensions, and many bridging-visa steps are done in **ImmiAccount** on the Department of Home Affairs website. AussieStart cannot lodge, pay, or track visas. This page only points to official URLs.

# Why this matters
Lookalike “visa agent” sites charge fees and steal passports. The real portal is on **homeaffairs.gov.au** / **online.immi.gov.au**.

# When to do it
When Home Affairs or your grant letter says you must apply, extend, or notify — not because a social-media ad created urgency.

# Requirements
- Passport and previous grant letter
- Credit card for official VAC (visa application charge) on the government site
- Time to complete biometrics if Home Affairs asks

# Step-by-step guide
1. Read the visa page that matches your situation on [immi.homeaffairs.gov.au](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au).
2. Create or sign in to [ImmiAccount](https://online.immi.gov.au/lusc/login). Check the address is `online.immi.gov.au`.
3. Check current conditions anytime with [VEVO](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/already-have-a-visa/check-visa-details-and-conditions/check-conditions-online).
4. If you use a registered migration agent, confirm they are on the [MARA register](https://www.mara.gov.au).
5. Never send passport scans to someone who messaged you first on WhatsApp.

# Common mistakes
- Paying “priority processing” to a non-government page
- Missing a deadline on a bridging visa while waiting for a friend to “handle it”
- Confusing VEVO (check conditions) with ImmiAccount (apply/manage)

# FAQs
**Can AussieStart be my migration agent?** No. We are not registered with MARA and do not give immigration advice.
**Where do I check processing times?** Home Affairs publishes them: [Visa processing times](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-processing-times).

# Overview
Enrolling your child in an Australian school is one of the first big family tasks after you arrive.

# Why this matters
School provides routine, English practice, friends, and a pathway into local life for the whole family.

# When to do it
As soon as you have a residential address — ideally before term starts, or within the first weeks of arrival.

# Requirements
Usually needed:
- Child’s passport and visa evidence
- Proof of address (lease, utility bill, or official letter)
- Immunisation history / statement
- Previous school reports (if available)
- Parent/guardian photo ID

# Step-by-step guide
1. Decide between government (public), Catholic, or independent schools.
2. Find your local intake school using your state education department finder.
3. Contact the school office and ask for enrolment forms.
4. Book an enrolment interview / school tour.
5. Submit documents and confirm start date, uniform, and bus/transport options.
6. Apply for any fee support or concession schemes you may be eligible for.

# Common mistakes
- Waiting until after term starts without contacting the school
- Assuming every school has open places
- Forgetting immunisation documents

# FAQs
**Are public schools free?** Tuition is generally free at government schools, but uniforms, camps, and some activities have costs.
**Can my child start mid-term?** Often yes — ask the school about vacancies and orientation support.

# Useful terms
- **Catchment / neighbourhood school**: The local public school linked to your address
- **Foundation / Prep / Kindergarten**: First year of formal schooling (name varies by state)

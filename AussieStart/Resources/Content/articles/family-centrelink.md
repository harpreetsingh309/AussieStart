# Overview
Centrelink (Services Australia) delivers payments and services some migrant families may be eligible for — depending on visa and residency rules.

# Why this matters
Understanding eligibility early prevents missed support for childcare, family tax benefits, or crisis help.

# When to do it
After you have a local address, phone, and myGov access — usually in the first 2–4 weeks.

# Requirements
Typical setup items:
- Passport / visa grant notice
- Arrival date and address
- Bank account (BSB + account number)
- TFN (when available)
- Relationship and child details

# Step-by-step guide
1. Create or sign in to **myGov** and link Centrelink.
2. Check payment eligibility for your visa subclass carefully.
3. Lodge only accurate claims — keep copies of documents uploaded.
4. Set up bank details and communication preferences.
5. Report changes (address, income, study/work) on time.

# Common mistakes
- Assuming every PR / temporary visa gets the same payments
- Missing reporting deadlines
- Sharing Centrelink logins or OTPs with anyone

# FAQs
**Do student visa holders get Centrelink?** Usually limited or not eligible for most payments — always check official rules for your visa.
**What is a CRN?** Customer Reference Number used across Services Australia.

# Useful terms
- **Waiting period**: Time you may need to wait before some payments start
- **Family Tax Benefit**: Possible support for eligible families with children

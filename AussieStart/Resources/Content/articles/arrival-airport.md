# Overview
Landing in Australia is exciting — and a bit overwhelming. This guide walks you through what happens after the plane doors open.

# Why this matters
Clearing immigration, collecting bags, and getting into the city calmly sets the tone for your first days.

# When to do it
As soon as you land — before you leave the airport terminal.

# Requirements
- Valid passport and visa grant notice (digital is fine)
- Arrival card / digital declaration if required for your flight
- Address of where you will stay for the first night
- Some Australian cash or a card that works internationally

# Step-by-step guide
1. Follow **Arrivals** signs to immigration / SmartGates.
2. Complete biosecurity screening and declare food, plant, or animal products.
3. Collect bags from the carousel matching your flight number.
4. Exit through customs to the public arrivals hall.
5. Buy a local SIM or connect airport Wi‑Fi, then book transport to your stay.

# Common mistakes
- Packing fresh food or seeds without declaring them
- Leaving the airport without a working phone number
- Forgetting your first-night address for forms and taxi drivers

# FAQs
**Do I need cash?** A little helps for small purchases, but cards are widely accepted.
**Can I use my overseas SIM?** Often yes with roaming — but a local prepaid SIM is usually cheaper within 24 hours.

# Useful terms
- **Biosecurity**: Rules protecting Australia from pests and diseases
- **SmartGate**: Automated passport gates for eligible travellers
